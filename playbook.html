About this data: I built this from public web data (HTTP Archive, October 2026), looking at the homepages of bigger, established brands, so the counts here are conservative. Store Leads tracks the whole ecommerce market and updates continuously: about 22,000 stores in mid-November with the AppLovin Pixel (the old Axon Pixel), versus 3.1M with Meta's. It counts every storefront, so a brand with several stores shows up more than once. This page counts each brand once and focuses on the established brands in that market. It skews US and Western; many Hong Kong and China cross-border sellers fall outside it. Benchmarks like supplements apply to this group, and the channel pattern is a correlation I use as a targeting signal. Named prospects and lighthouses were live-checked on Nov 16, 2026, with no AppLovin Pixel detected; category counts come from public data and are directional. Includes 26 big-brand adopters confirmed live on Nov 16 that public trackers missed. Categories are tagged automatically, so a few may be off. Not affiliated with AppLovin. Krishna Goli · LinkedIn
